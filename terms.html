CRM Backups Terms of Service

Effective date: 8 October 2026

Purpose and availability

CRM Backups is a private backup utility operated by the owner of this repository for their own self-hosted CRM systems. It uses rclone and restic to store encrypted backups in an authorized Google Drive account. It is not a public backup service and offers no public registration or paid subscription.

Authorized use

The utility is intended only for accounts and data that the operator owns or is authorized to manage. Google Drive access must be authorized by the account holder through Google OAuth. The informational website does not request visitors' Google credentials or accept CRM data.

Operator responsibilities

The operator is responsible for account security, lawful handling of source data, available storage, backup schedules, retention settings and protecting encryption passwords and OAuth credentials. Successful restoration depends on usable backups and access to the necessary recovery credentials.

Backup limitations

Backup jobs can fail because of connectivity, service limits, configuration errors or other technical problems. The utility does not promise uninterrupted operation, a particular retention period or guaranteed recovery. The operator should review job results and periodically test restoration before relying on a backup.

Third-party services

Google Drive, GitHub Pages, rclone and restic are separate products or services. Their applicable terms, privacy policies and software licenses remain in effect. This utility is not endorsed by Google or GitHub.

Privacy and stopping use

Data handling is described in the Privacy Policy. The operator can stop backup jobs and revoke Google Account access at any time. Revoking access does not remove existing cloud or local backups; those must be managed separately.

Contact and changes

For non-sensitive questions, use the repository issue tracker. Do not post passwords, tokens or private records. Changes to these terms will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
